New `@parity/product-sdk-chat-protocol`, experimental: the person-to-person chat protocol of the Polkadot apps over the statement store, as the person chat product runs it. The SCALE codecs of statements, frames, messages (with the `Unsupported` fallback that keeps raw bytes), chat requests, push payloads, HOP entries, data-channel setups and device sync; the key, topic, channel, push id, expiry and HOP derivations; the pairwise and multi-device lanes, the session state machine as a pure `transition`, its runtime `CommunicationSession`, `TopicSubscriptions` and `ContactSession`; chat requests; HOP compaction; and the call relay between a host call engine's signals and the native call messages. Everything that reaches the network goes through ports. `./host` implements them over the host (`hostStatementsPort`, `hostDirectory`, `heldStatements`, statement conversion); `./rooms` turns contents into Chat Extension v2 bodies (`contentBody`, `attachmentItem`, `attachmentOf`, call facts); `./keys` gives a product a chat identity of its own (`selfCustodiedChatKeys`, `keypairProver`); `./testing` has stand-ins for every port and the chat-spec Appendix D vectors as data with `checkVectors()`. `wire/scale` re-exports the `scale-ts` combinators: codecs that nest this package's codecs must be built from them, not from another copy of `scale-ts`.
