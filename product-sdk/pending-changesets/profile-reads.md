**Read a person's public profile from their DotNS name, prepare a change to it, and set a mood on it, through `@parity/product-sdk/profile`.**

A profile is one DotNS text record, `profile`, on the deployment's content resolver: a base64 SCALE `ProfileRecord` on the core's layout, so what this module reads is what hosts show. Index 0 is the core's `Avatar` (a Bulletin CID as its 32-byte digest, and a format: `png`, `jpeg`, `webp` or `gif`); this package proposes `Name` and `Feed`, reserves index 2 for pinned NFTs, and `PROFILE_ENTRY_INDEX` is where the next index is claimed. A reader keeps every entry it cannot read, and a writer puts it back.

- `getProfile(label, opts)` takes the same `DotNsClientOptions` as every DotNS read, accepts a DNS label or a dotted lite username such as `alice.01`, and answers `Found`, `NoProfile` (clear a cached profile) or `err` (keep it). `getProfileRecord` is the text as stored; `decodeProfileRecord` the decoder alone; `loadAvatar` fetches the bytes through the host's preimage lookup, never a gateway, and keeps them only if they hash to the CID.
- A write is read, change, write back, in the order the core's publisher keeps; the SDK prepares, the product maps and funds its account, signs and submits. `profileAvatar(bytes, format)` is the avatar entry, from the bytes' digest, with no host call; `rewriteProfileRecord(live, patch)` builds the new text, carrying every entry the patch does not name and refusing a live text that is not a profile (pass `null` to replace one); `prepareSetProfile(label, text, opts)` is the `setText` call, sized by a dry-run as `opts.origin`, which the registry must authorise for the name and which must be mapped in pallet-revive; `submitAvatar(bytes, manager)` then stores the bytes on Bulletin, after the prepare and before the submit; `verifyPublishedProfile` reads the record back after finality.
- Moods: the record's `mood` feed names a statement topic and a signer, and a mood is one statement there, signed by the host's statement allowance key, gone a day after it was set. `deriveMoodFeed` is the feed entry every publish writes; `setMood` sets or clears with no record write (`SignerMismatch` means publish first); `readMood` and `watchMood` answer the live mood, with `err` for a host that could not be read rather than "no mood". `MOOD_KINDS` and `MOOD_INTENSITIES` are the palette, fixed here and grown by appending.

`ProfileError` reasons: `NotBase64`, `TooLarge`, `Malformed`, `InvalidEntry`, `Host`, `SignerMismatch`, and `Timeout` for a host call whose deadline passed, which an avatar store or a mood submit may still outlive. The identity module gains `resolveDotNsContentResolver`, `DOTNS_CONTENT_RESOLVER_KEY`, the `contentResolverAddress` option and the `Unauthorised` reason on `DotNsError`; `setDotNsRecord` now refuses an undecodable origin as `InvalidOrigin` before any call, as `prepareDotNsRegistration` already did.
