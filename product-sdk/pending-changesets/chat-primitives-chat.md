New `@parity/product-sdk-chat`: build a chat product whose rooms the host draws through Chat Extension v2. `RoomDeclarations` creates rooms and declares their configuration, metadata, features, footer and contact, sending only what changed. `FeedSender` pushes feed updates in order per room at the core's 20 per second, sends a `RateLimited` update again, and folds consecutive edits of one message. `PreviewScheduler` sends one chat-list preview per burst, and `buildPreview` makes it from the newest message, unread count, newest reaction and call. Builders for messages and bodies (`message`, `text`, `richText`, `event`, `call`, `payment`, `replyPreview`), reactions (`reactionSummaries`, `reactionDetails`, `reactionChanges`) and edit histories. `page` and `searchPage` answer `messages_query` and `search_query` from any ordered source with opaque cursors. `serveRooms` routes every host query to the `RoomSource` of its room, `userActions` answers user actions with one handler per action and answers a repeated `NewMessage` or `SendPayment` with what was stored the first time, `streamMessage` writes a long answer as `New` `Preparing`, edits and `Sent`, and `defineChatWorker` registers the handlers before the first `await` and restarts the product on an identity change. `ROOM_LIMITS`, `ROOM_RATES`, `QUERY_WAITS_MS` and `checkMessage` carry the RFCs' bounds. `StoredRoom` serves a product-source room from a `RoomMessageStore` (`MemoryRoomMessageStore`, or `JournaledRoomMessageStore` in host storage, which opens from the indexes and reads a message when first asked for): it answers pages, user actions, who reacted, search and edit history, and pushes the feed update and schedules the preview on every `post`, `edit`, `react` and `setStatus`, handing what the user sends to `onUserMessage`. `sessionScope(context)` gives the storage prefix of the signed-in identity. `RoomCalls` runs one call at a time over a product's own `CallSignalling` channel: the room's `Ringing` and `Active` state, the call events, call media, early answers and the 30 s ring age, in the call-media RFC's flow (the side that accepts makes the media offer) or the native one (`offers: "caller"`). `openLinks()` routes what the user opened by prefix, and `hopResource`, `image`, `video` and `file` build media items. `StoredRoom.post(…, { notify })` sends the room's notification. The host bindings and Chat Extension v2 types are re-exported, and `@parity/product-sdk-chat/testing` has `createChatTestbed()`. `@parity/product-sdk` gains the `./chat` subpath.
