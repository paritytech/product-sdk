Add game registration in one call, for every product that signs players up: `registerForGame` reads the seat, binds the lite alias when the free route needs it, decides the prize-draw entries from the sign-up requirement at the best block, then hands the call, signed under the origin its route needs, to a `submit` capability. Registration is judged by the chain's clock, the sign-up resolves at the best block unless `waitFor: "finalized"` is asked for, a refused signature is never asked for again, and every outcome and `GameRegistrationError` carries a report and says which step failed and whether a retry can help (`registrationRetryDelay` gives the backoff). `readGameSeat` and the pure `deriveSeat` give the route (`returning`, `free`, `deposit` or `unfunded`) so every product shows the same answer, and `bindGameSeat` runs the bind leg alone: already-bound and doomed binds ask for no proof, and the `Game.LiteInvites` pin is checked before it goes out. `GAME_PRODUCT_NAME`, `GAME_ACCOUNT_INDEX` and `gameProductId` name the game account in one place. The new `@parity/product-sdk-individuality/host` entry builds the capabilities with `hostGameRegistrationCapabilities`, submitting through `@parity/product-sdk-tx`; it takes `@parity/product-sdk-host` and `@parity/product-sdk-tx` as optional peer dependencies so the main entry stays free of both.
